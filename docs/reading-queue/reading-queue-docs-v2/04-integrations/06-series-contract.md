# Series Contract

## Series relation

Reading Queue can include books from series.

Queue item may show series info:

- series title;
- part number;
- reading status in series context if available.

## Important

Adding a book to Reading Queue does not automatically add next series books.

Automatic add next books from series is not part of MVP.

## Reorder

Queue order is independent from series order.

User can place series books in any queue order.
